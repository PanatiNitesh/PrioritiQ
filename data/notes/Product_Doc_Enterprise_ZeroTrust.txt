PrioritiQ Zero-Trust Architecture guarantees that customer CRM data, email streams, and transcripts remain encrypted in-transit (TLS 1.3) and at-rest (AES-256). Enterprise tier includes VPC peering, HIPAA BAA compliance, SOC2 Type II audited perimeter, and customer-managed KMS keys. Deployments can run fully on-premises or via dedicated AWS/Azure private endpoints without external model training leakage.
